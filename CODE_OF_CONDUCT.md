# Code of Conduct

We expect all contributors and community members to follow these guidelines.

## Our Pledge
We pledge to be respectful, inclusive, and considerate. Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory behavior is not tolerated.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainers:
- Email: conduct@basicmails.example (replace with real contact)
- Or open a confidential issue.

## Enforcement
Reports will be reviewed by the maintainers and may result in warnings, removal of privileges, or banning from the community, depending on severity.
