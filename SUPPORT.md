# Support

Need help? Here are the recommended ways to get it.

## Community Support
- Open an issue: use the `bug` or `question` templates.
- Discussions (if enabled): ask questions and share use-cases.

## Commercial / Priority Support
If you need enterprise or priority support, email: support@basicmails.example (replace with real contact) and include:
- Repo & version
- Steps to reproduce
- Logs and environment info

## Troubleshooting Checklist
1. Check logs (`docker logs` or app logs).
2. Confirm env variables (SMTP host, API keys).
3. Search issues and docs.
